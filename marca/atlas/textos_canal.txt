History and geography, one map at a time.

Why do almost all Egyptians live along one river? Why are borders drawn the way they are? Why did empires rise exactly where they did? Every map hides a story — about rivers, deserts, mountains and the people who shaped the world around them.

World Through Time explains history and geography with clear animated maps, real data and stories you won't forget.

New videos every week. Subscribe and see how the past shaped the map — and the map shaped the past.

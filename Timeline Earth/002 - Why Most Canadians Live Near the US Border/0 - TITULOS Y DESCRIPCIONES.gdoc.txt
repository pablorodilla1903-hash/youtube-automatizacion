DESCRIPCIÓN DEL VÍDEO
Canada is the second largest country on Earth, yet it has roughly the same population as California — and most of those people live in a thin strip close to the United States. Why?

In this video: how cold, permafrost and the rocky Canadian Shield decide where people can live, why the St. Lawrence River and the Great Lakes shaped the first settlements, how a railway built in 1885 created Canada's western cities, and why the pull of the United States still keeps Canadians close to the border.

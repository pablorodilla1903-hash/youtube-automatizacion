DESCRIPCIÓN DEL VÍDEO
Australia is almost as big as the mainland United States, yet it has only around twenty-seven million people, and most of them live in a handful of cities on the coast. Why is the middle of the continent almost empty?

In this video: why Australia is the driest inhabited continent, how mountains and sinking air steal its rain, how Aboriginal peoples lived in the desert for tens of thousands of years, why the colonies grew as ports, the explorers and the telegraph line that crossed the outback, Goyder's Line, and life in the outback today.

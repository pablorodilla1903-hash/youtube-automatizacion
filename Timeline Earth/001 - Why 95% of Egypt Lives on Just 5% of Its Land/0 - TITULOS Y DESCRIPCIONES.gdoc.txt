DESCRIPCIÓN DEL VÍDEO
Egypt is roughly the size of France and Spain put together, yet about 95% of its more than 100 million people live on a thin green strip covering around 5% of the country. Why?

In this video: how a river that starts thousands of kilometers away crosses the Sahara, why its yearly flood created one of the first great civilizations in history, how Egypt moved the temples of Abu Simbel to save them from the Aswan High Dam, and why the Nile is now at the center of a conflict with Ethiopia.

DESCRIPCIÓN DEL VÍDEO
Mexico City, one of the largest cities in the world, is sinking, in some areas by tens of centimeters every year. How can a whole city sink?

In this video: the Aztec capital built on an island in a lake, how the Spanish and later governments drained that lake, the soft clay left behind, the groundwater pumped from beneath the city, why a city built on a lake now struggles both with floods and with water shortages, and what can be done.

DESCRIPCIÓN DEL VÍDEO
Venice is a city of palaces, churches and squares built in the middle of a shallow lagoon, on more than a hundred small islands. Why would anyone build a city on water?

In this video: the refugees who fled to the marshes, how millions of wooden piles hold the city up, how a town of fishermen and salt makers became the richest trading power in Europe, how Venetians moved whole rivers to save their lagoon, and the giant barriers that now protect it from the sea.

DESCRIPCIÓN DEL VÍDEO
For more than two thousand years, armies, fleets and empires fought to control one city: Byzantium, then Constantinople, now Istanbul. Why this one?

In this video: the Bosphorus, the narrow strait between Europe and Asia that controls the only sea route out of the Black Sea, the Greek colonists who chose the site, Constantine's New Rome, walls that held for a thousand years, the fall of the city in 1453, why Russia and Britain fought over the straits, and why the strait still matters today.
